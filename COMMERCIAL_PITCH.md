# Commercial Pitch: SKU-Level Demand Forecasting Engine

## Problem Statement
FMCG distributors, kirana aggregators, and D2C brands lose ₹10-30 crore annually due to:
- Stockouts during festivals (Diwali, Pongal, Eid)
- Overstocking of slow-moving SKUs
- Manual Excel-based forecasting with 40-60% error rates
- No visibility into demand drivers

## Solution
AI-powered demand forecasting engine that predicts 4-8 week SKU-level demand with:
- 85%+ accuracy (WRMSSE < 0.60)
- Indian festival calendar integration
- Explainable demand drivers per SKU
- Automated reorder point recommendations

## Technology Moat
1. **Indian Festival Intelligence**: Pre-trained on Diwali, Pongal, Ugadi, Dasara, Eid patterns
2. **Hybrid Models**: NeuralProphet + LightGBM for superior accuracy
3. **Explainability**: SHAP-based drivers ("Diwali adding +340 units vs baseline")
4. **M5 Benchmark**: Validated on Walmart's 42,840 time series dataset
